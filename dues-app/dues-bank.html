Step 1 — the first sync

Reads the chapter account through Plaid. Only money coming in is ever stored; the chapter’s own spending is counted and thrown away before it reaches the database.

What the bank sent

Plaid reports a checking account with positive meaning money out, so every amount is flipped on the way in. Get that backwards and every dues payment is filed as chapter spending and silently dropped.

Applied without asking you

Only when the sender can be exactly one brother and the amount is exactly what he still owes. Each one records why, and undo puts the credit back in the queue.

Taken back by the bank

reversed automatically

A return written in a descriptor is a guess about text, so it waits for you. The bank reporting a transaction removed is not a guess — it is the bank saying the money never arrived, so it reverses on its own rather than sitting credited overnight.
